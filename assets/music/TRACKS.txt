The Twelfth Moon: the music it plays
=====================================

Save each track in this folder under the name on the left, as .ogg (or .mp3).
The game picks it up by itself (open the project in Godot once so it imports
the file). A track that isn't here yet is silent, or plays the stand-in named
in brackets.

Where to look: opengameart.org > Browse > Music, and put the words after
"Tags" in the search's Tags box (a few at a time). Prefer licences CC0,
CC-BY 3.0/4.0 or OGA-BY; write down the title, the author, the licence and
the page's link for every track you use: they all go in the credits.
Prefer orchestral, piano or acoustic music over chiptune (8-bit) to match the
art. Loops ("loop" in the tags or the title) are best for everything except
the reveal and the ending. Keep each file under about 3 MB, so the game still
loads fast on a phone.

MUST HAVE (all in since 2026-10-01; Kael's fight and the credits share the
boss and title tracks for now; who made each is in CREDITS.txt)
---------
title.ogg           Title screen.
                    Gentle and a little mysterious: a red moon, a love story.
                    Tags: music box, lullaby, piano, calm, romantic, mysterious, fantasy

memory.ogg          The prologue and all four past-life memories (one shared theme).
                    The heart of the game: an old memory, sweet and sad.
                    Tags: music box, nostalgia, melancholy, sad, waltz, dreamy

village.ogg         Chapter One, the village by day.
                    Warm, peaceful, folk: flute, lute or acoustic guitar.
                    Tags: village, town, peaceful, pastoral, folk, medieval, happy, flute

lantern_forest.ogg  Chapter Two, the forest at night with lanterns and fireflies.
                    Magical and calm, a bit of wonder: harp, bells, soft pads.
                    Tags: forest, night, magical, mysterious, calm, ambient, fantasy, harp

boss.ogg            The three mini-bosses (Moon Witch, Shield Knight, Grave Warden).
                    Exciting but not grim; she should feel brave, not scared.
                    Tags: boss, battle, fight, action, epic, orchestral, fantasy

long_road.ogg       Chapter Three, the autumn road (fields, farm, golden wood).
                    A journey: hopeful and adventurous.
                    Tags: adventure, journey, travel, overworld, uplifting, folk, autumn

ember_keep.ogg      Chapter Four, inside the castle.
                    Tense and dark, building suspense: low strings, drums.
                    Tags: castle, dungeon, dark, tense, suspense, mysterious

rooftop.ogg         Chapter Five, the roof in a storm under a huge red moon.
                    The last climb: dramatic, a storm, rising tension, choir.
                    Tags: epic, dramatic, storm, final, climax, orchestral, choir

kael_sword.ogg      The fight with Kael (the Ember Knight).
                    The final boss, but tragic: epic and sad at once.
                    Tags: final boss, epic, tragic, emotional, orchestral, battle

reveal.ogg          He kneels, she remembers, "Happy birthday, Mariane". Plays once.
                    Solo piano: tender, heartbreaking, romantic, slow.
                    Tags: piano, sad, emotional, romantic, tender, slow
                    (Mayra Gandra's free emotional piano pack on itch.io is a good fit;
                    credit required.)

credits.ogg         The end screen and the credits.
                    Warm and bittersweet, a love theme: piano and strings.
                    Tags: credits, ending, love, romantic, warm, piano, strings, bittersweet
