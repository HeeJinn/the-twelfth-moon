Music credits (for the game's credits screen)
=============================================

Every track below must be credited unless it is CC0 (credit is still kind).
"To confirm": the author and licence still need checking on the track's
OpenGameArt page (the file carried no tags). Changes made: the MP3 and OGG
tracks were re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis at about 112 kbps (tools/shrink_music.py),
"A Lucid Dream" (reveal) keeps its first three minutes with a fade out, and
"Together" is compressed by Godot (QOA) and set to loop.
