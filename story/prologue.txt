# Prologue: a memory from a thousand years ago.
# Same format as chapter_01.txt. "Her" and "Him" show as "???".

[prologue_opening]
Once in a thousand years, a red moon rises. The Twelfth Moon.
On the twelfth night of the tenth month, it falls on the one soul it has marked.

[prologue_1]
Her: Look... the moon is turning red.
Him: Don't look at it. Look at me.
Her: It's choosing someone. I can feel it.
Her: Promise me something.
Him: Anything.
Her: If it takes me tonight... find me again.
Him: I promise. In this life, and the next, and every one after.

[prologue_2]
Him: No... no, no, no.
Him: I'll find you. I swear it.
Him: And next time, I'll be the one who falls.

[prologue_wake]
Tomas: Mariane... Mariane!
