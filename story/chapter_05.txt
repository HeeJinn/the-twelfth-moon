# Chapter Five: the Twelfth Night
#
# Same format as chapter_03.txt. Speakers: Mariane, Kael (shown as "The Ember Knight"),
# and Ember (Kael once she remembers him). Plain lines are narration.
# Numbers as words: the font has no digits.

[thought_roof]
Mariane: The roof of the keep. The storm is sitting right on top of us.
Mariane: And the moon... it's so close I could touch it.

[thought_towers]
Mariane: Every night of the twelve led here.
Mariane: Grandpa, Rosa, Pell, Hild... I promised all of them I would come home.

[thought_summit]
Mariane: He's up there, with his back to me, looking at the moon.
Mariane: Like someone saying goodbye.

[kael_intro]
Kael: You came. Twelve nights, and not once did you turn back.
Mariane: You burned our shrine. You sent your soldiers into my village.
Kael: I did.
Mariane: Then it ends tonight, Ember Knight.
Kael: Yes. Tonight it ends.
Kael: Show me how strong you've grown. Guard when my sword rises, and strike when it falls.

[kael_again]
Kael: Again. Guard when my sword rises. Strike when it falls.

[kael_fire]
Kael: Good. You were always stronger than you knew.
Kael: Now the fire. It falls only where the rings glow. Step toward me, and it cannot touch you.
Mariane: ...Why would you tell me that?
Kael: ...

[reveal]
The red moon's light pours down into him, and everything comes back at once.
Mariane: ...Ember?
Ember: You remembered. That's more than I hoped for.
Mariane: Why? The monsters, the shrines...
Ember: The moon marks one soul. I just changed which one.
Ember: It had to be your hand, Mariane. I needed you strong enough to reach me... and angry enough to finish it.
Ember: Last time, I watched the sky take you. This time, you get to live.
Ember: Happy birthday, Mariane.

[reveal_after]
Mariane: The last petal...
Mariane: Ember. You kept your promise. You found me.
