# Chapter One: The Village
#
# Every line of dialogue in this chapter. Edit freely.
#   [name]          starts a conversation (the game refers to it by name)
#   Speaker: text   a spoken line
#   text            a line with no speaker is narration
#   [name_again]    what someone says if you talk to them a second time
# Speakers: Mariane, Tomas, Rosa, Lina, Mara, Joss, Bram, Theo, Kael.
# Keep lines short (about two lines in the box). The font has no digits,
# so write numbers as words.

[wake_up]
Tomas: Mariane! There you are, asleep under the blossom tree again.
Mariane: Grandpa Tomas... I had that dream again.
Mariane: The flowering tree. A red moon. Someone holding my hand.
Tomas: Hmph. Too many of Old Mara's stories before bed, if you ask me.
Tomas: Now, be a dear and take the morning offering to the shrine.
Tomas: It's at the east end of the village, past the wheat fields.
Mariane: Of course. I'll be back before lunch.
Tomas: And keep your sword close. Joss swears he saw goblins out there.

[lina]
Lina: Mariane! Twelve more days until your birthday, right?
Lina: I'm baking you a strawberry cake. Don't you dare peek!
Mariane: I would never. ...Maybe a little.
[lina_again]
Lina: No peeking! Go on, the shrine won't visit itself.

[rosa]
Rosa: Morning, sweetheart! Stew's on, warm from the fire.
Rosa: Did you see the moon last night? It had a red ring around it.
Rosa: My mother used to say that means the moon is choosing someone.
[rosa_again]
Rosa: Don't mind my old wives' tales. Eat something before you go!

[mara]
Mara: Come here, child. Let an old woman tell you a story.
Mara: Once in a thousand years, a red moon rises. The Twelfth Moon.
Mara: On the twelfth night of the tenth month, it falls on the soul it has marked.
Mariane: The twelfth night of the tenth month... That's my birthday.
Mara: Is it now? Then you'd best make a very good wish, dear.
[mara_again]
Mara: Stories are just stories. Mostly.

[theo]
Theo: Morning, Mariane! Off to the shrine again?
Theo: Say a prayer for my wheat, would you? The crows are bold this year.
[theo_again]
Theo: If you see any crows, tell them I said shoo.

[bram]
Bram: Hold up, Mariane. The road east isn't safe today.
Bram: Something has the monsters stirred up. More of them every night.
Mariane: The offering can't wait, Bram. I'll be careful.
Bram: Stubborn as your grandpa. Light the campfires along the road, they'll keep you safe.
[bram_again]
Bram: I'll hold the village. You watch yourself out there.

[joss]
Joss: Mariane! Goblins! A whole pack of them, in the wheat fields!
Joss: They chased me all the way from the scarecrow!
Mariane: Get inside, Joss. I'll handle them.

[shrine_arrive]
Mariane: The shrine... Why is the air so warm?

[shrine_kael]
Mariane: Hey! Get away from the shrine!
Kael: ...
Kael: Twelve nights, Mariane.
Mariane: How do you know my name?

[shrine_after]
Tomas: Mariane! Are you hurt? I saw the fire from the village.
Mariane: A knight in red armor. He took the shard from the shrine.
Tomas: The Ember Knight... So the old stories were true.
Tomas: He's gathering the moon's shards. When he has them all, the Twelfth Moon will fall.
Tomas: On the twelfth night. Your birthday, child.
Mariane: Then I won't let him have them. I'll find him and take them back.
Tomas: ...You always did have your grandmother's fire.
Tomas: Take the forest road, and come home safe. Promise me.
Mariane: I promise.
