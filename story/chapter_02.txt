# Chapter Two: The Lantern Forest
#
# Same format as chapter_01.txt. Speakers: Mariane, Wren, Pell, Witch.
# Numbers as words: the font has no digits.

[wren]
Wren: Oh! A traveler, this late? You must be very brave, or very lost.
Mariane: I'm following a knight in red armor. Have you seen him?
Wren: The Ember Knight... He came through here three nights ago.
Wren: Strangest thing. He lit every lantern along the road as he went.
Wren: I'm the lantern keeper, you see. That's my job. He did it for me.
Mariane: A villain who lights lanterns?
Wren: The forest is restless tonight, dear. Follow the lanterns and don't stray.
[wren_again]
Wren: Follow the lanterns. They'll show you the way.

[pell]
Pell: Careful, friend! The clearing ahead... something's in there. A witch, I think.
Pell: She's been casting spells all night. Like she's waiting for someone.
Mariane: Waiting for whom?
Pell: Didn't ask. I'm rather fond of my eyebrows.
Pell: If she throws those purple lights, duck! Or slide, or jump. Just don't stand there.
[pell_again]
Pell: Purple lights: duck, slide or jump. And hit her while she's catching her breath!

[witch_intro]
Witch: So you're the one. Smaller than I expected.
Mariane: Where is the Ember Knight? What is he planning?
Witch: Planning? Hah. You'll have to earn your answers, girl.
Witch: Show me you can survive the moon's own magic!

[witch_again]
Witch: Back already? Good. Again!

[witch_defeated]
Witch: Enough... enough. You're stronger than you look.
Mariane: Why did you attack me? Who sent you?
Witch: He told us to make you stronger... not to break you.
Witch: Strange orders, for a villain.
Mariane: He... what?
Witch: The ember road runs north, to the winter pass. He'll be waiting. He always is.

[witch_after]
Mariane: Make me stronger...?
Mariane: Why would he want that?
