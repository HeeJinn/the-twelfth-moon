# The petal journal: one line of her past life for each petal she finds,
# in the order she finds them (the twenty-first falls from Ember at the end).
# One line per petal, in story order. A line starting with "#" is a note.
# Keep each line short: a page holds about fifteen letters across.
# Numbers as words: the font has no digits.
Spring. A tree in bloom, and me.
The smith took a new apprentice.
He burned his fingers every day.
I called him Ember. He liked it.
He walked me home from the well.
He made me a ring of bent nails.
We named the stars from the forge roof.
Summer. He taught me the sword.
I beat him at it. He laughed.
Winter. A ribbon, red as his forge.
So he could find me in the snow.
Autumn. The old women spoke of a red moon.
I dreamed of it. It watched me.
He said: let it look at me instead.
Anything can be reforged, he said.
Three nights left. He went quiet.
Under our tree, he made a promise.
This life, and the next, and every one after.
The moon turned red. Find me, I said.
It took me. He held on to the end.
He found me. He kept his promise.
