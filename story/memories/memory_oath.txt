# A past-life memory, after Chapter Four. "Her" and "Him" show as "???".

[memory_opening]
A memory. Autumn, a thousand years ago. Three nights before the red moon.

[memory_oath]
Him: You've been quiet all day. Even the sparks can't make you laugh.
Her: The old women at the well say the red moon is coming back. That it marks someone first.
Her: Ember... I think it's marked me. I dream of it every night, and it's always looking at me.
Him: Then I'll stand in front of you. Let it look at me instead.
Her: You can't fight the moon with a hammer, silly.
Him: Watch me. Anything can be reforged. I'll find a way.
Her: Then promise me something. Whatever happens, don't do anything foolish.
Him: ...
Her: Ember. Promise.
Him: I promise I'll find you. That one I know I can keep.

[memory_wake]
Mariane: "Let it look at me instead."
Mariane: The moon watches him now. Ember... what did you do?
