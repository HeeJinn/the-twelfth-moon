# A past-life memory, after Chapter Three. "Her" and "Him" show as "???".

[memory_opening]
A memory. Winter, a thousand years ago. The solstice fair.

[memory_ribbon]
Her: Ember! You'll freeze out here. What are you hiding behind your back?
Him: It's... for you. For the fair.
Her: A ribbon? Red as your forge.
Her: Ember, this must have cost a whole month of horseshoes.
Him: Two months. ...So I can always find you in the snow. Wherever you are.
Her: Then you'd better always look for me.
Him: Always.

[memory_wake]
Mariane: This ribbon... Grandpa says I've worn one since I was little.
Mariane: "So I can always find you." ...Who were you?
