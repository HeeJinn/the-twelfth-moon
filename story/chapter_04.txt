# Chapter Four: Ember Keep
#
# Same format as chapter_03.txt. Speaker: Mariane. Numbers as words: the font has no digits.

[thought_gate]
Mariane: The Ember Keep. He went in ahead of me.
Mariane: The moon looks bigger from here. As if the whole sky leaned closer to watch.

[thought_lifts]
Mariane: Stone lifts, still turning. Someone keeps them oiled.
Mariane: Someone who expected company.

[thought_vents]
Mariane: Embers that glow and ring before they burst. Every trap in this place warns me first.
Mariane: Who builds a castle like that?

[thought_windows]
Mariane: Every window holds the red moon, and every one is bigger than the last.
Mariane: It isn't watching me. It's watching him.

[thought_stair]
Mariane: The stair goes down, under the keep. The moon's light bleeds through the stone.
Mariane: Whatever he's hiding, it's down there.

[thought_undercroft]
Mariane: Under the keep the stone is soft and warm, like something breathing.
Mariane: The moon's light got in here somehow. It soaked into everything.

[thought_entity]
Mariane: It opened its eyes. But it isn't watching me.
Mariane: It's watching the way back up. Toward him.

[thought_altar]
Mariane: A book, left open on the altar. Every page says the same thing, in the same hand.
Mariane: "In every life."
